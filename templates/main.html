Welcome to LibNexa

A smart, intuitive platform designed to simplify and modernize library management. From tracking books and managing student records to generating clearance certificates, LibNexa brings every essential library task into one streamlined system.

Developed by Kunal Das, Ashim Bakshi, and Arindam Das — final-year students of the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETCE), Falakata Polytechnic.

About LibNexa

LibNexa is your all-in-one library management solution designed to simplify and digitize every aspect of library operations. Our mission is to replace outdated manual processes with a smart, efficient system that supports real-time tracking, accurate reporting, and easy access to information.


From book issuance and returns to student record management, overdue tracking, payments, and clearance certificates — LibNexa empowers you with everything you need in one seamless platform.
